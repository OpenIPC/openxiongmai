# libisp_XM530

An open `libisp_XM530.so` for the XM530/XM550: the ISP library majestic
loads next to `libispapp_XM530.so` and the sensor library, in place of the
vendor's (issue #76). It is laid out as openhisilicon's `libraries/isp`:
`src/main` for what any chip shares, `arch/xm530` for this chip's tables. It
builds against the SDK headers in `libraries/isp/include`, as `libispapp`
does.

```sh
make CROSS_COMPILE=arm-openipc-linux-musleabi-
```

## What it does (M1, first part)

- **Exports** every function majestic, `libispapp_XM530` and the sensor
  libraries import from the vendor library: the `XM_MPI_ISP_*` calls, the
  `XM_MPI_VDA_*` calls they use, `Read_IspReg`/`Write_IspReg`,
  `ispMemnCpy`, `SysDelay_ms`, `IspSetSlowFrameRate`, `Mipi_check_*`.
  `ispMemnCpy` takes the source first, as the vendor's does: majestic keeps
  its MIPI attributes with it, and the other way round its MIPI start after
  `Init` took the IMX335 for a DVP sensor and left the receiver stopped.
- **Registers** go through `/dev/isp` (`_IOW('I', 1/2, 12)`), the system
  controller through `/dev/mem`.
- **3A:** AE and AWB are majestic's. `MemInit` takes their functions from
  `XM_MPI_ISP_AELibRegCallBack`/`AWBLibRegCallBack` and gives each its handle
  (`ctrl` command 1). `Init` runs their inits and lays the AWB statistics out
  over the window `awb_init` returns. `Run` starts a thread that, every
  frame, runs AWB (it reads the statistics itself, through `Read_IspReg`, and
  its gains and colour matrix go to `AWB_BASE` and the CSM), makes the luma
  grids AE takes from what AWB read, and runs AE (it sets the sensor itself;
  its ISP gain goes to `DIGITAL_GAIN`). `tools/xm-isp-trace/README.md` has
  the interface as traced.
- **Start-up, for any sensor:** the vendor library starts every sensor the
  same way, but for four things, and the library does the same
  (`arch/xm530/xm530_start.h`):
  - **a template** of its writes and attributes (`xm530_start.c`);
  - **the geometry**, computed from the picture size
    (`xm530_geometry.c`): the 3D noise reduction's planes in an "Isp" MMZ
    block of its own and their line strides, and the local tone mapping
    grid;
  - **the sensor's defaults**, which the sensor library gives through
    `pfn_cmos_get_isp_default`: the AGC table (sharpening, 2D and 3D NR,
    defect pixels, saturation and black level by ISO, and the Y gamma that
    goes into CSC contrast) and the gamma curve;
  - **what the vendor library has built in for some sensors**, by the
    `SENSOR_CHIP` id their library registers: SharpenV2 and 3DNrV2 tables
    (IMX335, SC5239, SC4236, SC335E), a DRC curve (SC5239, SC4236), and
    sharpening registers in the first `Run` pass (F37, SP2305, MIS2003,
    OV9732).

  `xm530_start.c` is generated by `tools/xm-isp-trace/start530.py` from
  traces of the vendor library, the lab IMX335's and those of every sensor
  the X50 and X123 sensor libraries start (26: IMX335, IMX307, IMX323,
  SC1235, SC2145, SC2145H, SC2235, SC2239, SC2335, SC3035, SC307E, SC3335,
  SC335E, SC4236, SC5235, SC5239, SC5332, K03, F37, H62, H65, AUGE,
  SP140A, SP2305, MIS2003, OV9732), taken in qemu-bench; it fails
  unless each trace is the template with that sensor's geometry, defaults
  and overrides. Run in qemu-bench on each sensor, the library's start-up
  registers and attributes are the vendor library's
  (`tools/xm-isp-trace/compare530.py`). `make test` checks the geometry
  against the vendor library at 40 picture sizes.
- **Attributes** are kept and returned. The gamma curve takes effect at once,
  and a saturation of 0 makes the picture black and white (Cb/Cr gain 0), as
  with the vendor library.
- **Tuning by gain** (`src/main/isp_tune.c`): every 7 frames or so, as the
  vendor library's loop, from the attributes' auto tables (one entry an ISO
  octave from 100, interpolated linearly in ISO): 2D noise reduction and
  the sharpening curve that eases off with it, sharpening (the SharpenV2
  algorithm's registers where a sensor's attributes choose it), black
  level, Cb/Cr gain, dynamic defect pixels and DRC (its
  strength from the defog table, its level the largest local tone mapping
  statistic), the luma curve once, and 3D noise reduction. The vendor
  library's 3D NR rule also depends on how the gain got there, but where
  the gain has settled an ISO always gets the same setting, so 3D NR is a
  measured schedule (`arch/xm530/xm530_imx335_nr3d.c`, generated by
  `tools/xm-isp-trace/nr3d530.py`), the nearest ISO's taken. Each was
  fitted against the vendor library at eleven light levels (ISO 100 to
  12800), and checked with the gain held in qemu-bench on 20 sensors: of
  the 18,270 register values where the gain had settled, 18,119 are the
  vendor library's, the rest one off where an entry is rounded.
- **Chroma and anti-false-colour** (`src/main/isp_color.c`) take effect at
  once, as with the vendor library: the R/G/B offsets, the 65-entry hue LUT
  from the six colour strengths (`arch/xm530/xm530_chroma.c`, generated by
  `tools/xm-isp-trace/chroma530.py` from a `chromasweep.so` log), and the
  anti-false-colour strength. Swept over every value on the lab XM550, the
  registers are the vendor library's, byte for byte.
- **Motion detection** (`XM_MPI_VDA_*`, `src/main/mpi_vda.c`) on the AE
  library's 7 × 7 grid, as the vendor library behaves: every `MoDframe + 1`
  grids the grid is compared with the last analysed one, a zone moved when it
  differs by more than its 1/`MoRatio`; `MoVdaBmark` marks every moved zone,
  the share of moved zones among `MoWinset`'s is returned (and sets
  `MoReflag` from `u8RatioThresh`), and results gather until read. On the lab
  XM550 it answers bulb changes and a blinking LED's zone as the vendor
  library does. Cover detection never marks a cover: on the lab XM550 the
  vendor library's marked nothing through darkness and a black or white
  sensor test pattern either (its focus value, `XM_MPI_VDA_Afgetdata`,
  returned -1), and enabled without `XM_MPI_VDA_CreatChn` and
  `XM_MPI_VDA_VdaApInit` first, it killed the process with SIGFPE.
- **Restarts:** majestic's AE and AWB keep their state, and AE its last
  statistics, in the block `/dev/isp` shares, at the vendor library's places,
  so a restarted majestic starts AE where it was, as with the vendor library.

## Open AE (M2, first part)

With `LIBISP_OPEN_AE=1` in majestic's environment, the library runs its own
auto-exposure in place of majestic's (`src/main/isp_ae.c`, on libxmisp's
`ae.c`). It is off by default; AWB stays majestic's.

- **What it holds:** what the vendor AE holds, measured on the lab XM550:
  the mean of the 7 × 7 luma grid weighted by the exposure attributes'
  table, over 256 (the "average luminance", 0-255), within the attributes'
  tolerance of their compensation. Majestic moves the compensation with the
  light (55 in a lit room, 51 to 33 as it darkens), and the attributes are
  read every frame through `XM_MPI_ISP_GetExposureAttr`, so it follows.
  Anti-flicker follows them too.
- **The sensor:** its library hands the open ISP its AE and AWB callbacks
  (`libisp_ae_sensor_register`, `libisp_awb_sensor_register`, weak calls
  the X50 and X123 libraries make after registering with majestic; absent
  with the vendor ISP library, which is unaffected). Lines come first, then
  the sensor's analog and digital gain at its nearest step, then the ISP's
  gain for the rest.
- **Majestic:** the result goes where the vendor AE keeps its own, in the AE
  state the library owns: lines, the three gains, the exposure and the
  average luminance. So `/metrics`, automatic day/night and the
  compensation majestic sets see the open AE's exposure.
- **Sensors:** it runs where the sensor's gains are in 1024-for-1x units,
  in its limits and its gain tables, which it checks at start: by their
  defaults the IMX335, IMX307, SC2239, SC2335 and F37. A sensor in other
  units (Auge's digital gain takes 128 for 1x, MIS2003's analog 64) keeps
  majestic's AE, and the log says why.
- **Majestic's settings:** the exposure-time range, the gain ranges and
  anti-flicker are read every frame, a new frame rate takes the sensor's
  line limit with it, and an exposure a narrowed limit no longer allows is
  planned again. Manual or bypassed exposure goes to majestic's AE, which
  does it; the open AE takes over again from where it left the exposure.
- **Not yet:** slow shutter and the "exposure is at its most" flag. Only the
  IMX335 has run on a camera.

Against majestic's AE on the lab XM550 (IMX335, 2560x1440 H.264), each
settled in turn under the same light:

| Bulbs | Majestic's AE: gain, `avelum` | Open AE: gain, `avelum` |
|---|---|---|
| 100% | 9.1x, ISP 1.02, 50 | 9.5x, ISP 1.00, 50 |
| 30% | 61x, ISP 1.00, 35 | 61x, ISP 1.01, 35 |
| 5% | 61x, ISP 1.00, 30 | 61x, ISP 1.04, 31 |
| off | 61x, ISP 1.07, 29 | 61x, ISP 1.09, 31 |

A step from dark to the bulbs settled within a second with either. After a
restart the open AE starts from the last run's exposure and settled in
about 6 s, majestic's AE in about 9 s, from black.

## Not yet

- 3D NR is a measured schedule for each sensor (`arch/xm530/xm530_nr3d.c`;
  the IMX335's from the lab camera, the rest in qemu-bench with the gain
  held by `tools/xm-isp-trace/statfake.so`), the vendor library's setting
  where the gain has settled. Its own rule also follows the picture's luma
  histogram and how the gain got there, which a schedule doesn't.
- Sensors other than the lab's IMX335 are checked in emulation only. With two or more chroma colours away from neutral, 3%
  of the hue LUT's entries differ from the vendor library's.

## Testing on a camera

Majestic stopped, the library bind-mounted over the vendor one:

```sh
mount --bind /tmp/libisp_XM530.so /usr/lib/libisp_XM530.so
LIBISP_DEBUG=1 majestic    # every 50 frames: grids, AWB gains, AE result
```

On the lab XM550 (IMX335, 4000 K bulbs), restarted after the vendor library,
it settles on the same sensor gain and frame rate, and the picture's mean
R/G/B is within 2 of the vendor library's at 100%, 30% and 2% light. Both
libraries start AE from the last run's state: compare one restart against
another, or two cold boots, since from a cold boot AE starts from nothing
and in dim light may drop to a slower frame rate.
